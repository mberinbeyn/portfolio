Overview

Yandex is the leading digital ecosystem of products for life in Eastern Europe with more than 60M daily users. Internal products for employees lived on different platforms with separate domains, so people couldn't find them and rarely used them. Each service was built differently, without a shared design system, which made delivery slow and support expensive. That's why the strategic goal was to build a unified Intranet — one door to all internal products, one entry point for every service on desktop and mobile.

Contribution

I led the discovery and strategy work for the Intranet. I studied the existing internal services to find where the differences between them caused the most problems for users. I also created the main idea—"one door to all internal products"—and used it to bring teams together around a shared direction. Finally, I defined the design principles that became the standard for consistency across all services in the ecosystem.

Results

The strategy created a shared language and common goal across teams, aligning different product groups around one direction for the Intranet. The consistency principles reduced design and development time by allowing teams to reuse patterns, and lowered UX fragmentation across services, giving the ecosystem one unified visual language.

Employee Profile · Time on page +42%, Return rate +18%

Redesigned the profile with clearer hierarchy, quick actions, and status indicators. Ran two usability rounds with employees to validate structure before handoff, resulting in higher engagement and repeat visits.

Design system · 40+ components, 3 platforms, 6 teams

Built a shared component library aligned with the new Intranet design language. Standardized tokens, typography, and interaction patterns to speed up delivery across teams and reduce inconsistencies.

User interviews · 8 participants, 30 min, 10 open questions

Conducted interviews to identify key user scenarios and uncover underrated features. Insights included: users want quick access to contacts, check availability for meetings, and need to locate colleagues in the office.

Learnings

What this work taught me about product and process

This project showed me that even with tight deadlines, it's possible to deliver impactful results by staying focused and collaborating closely with the team. If I were to start over, I would put more effort into optimizing and unifying components early on to improve consistency and reduce future work.

I also discovered the RITE method for user research, which I continue to use in my practice. I gained hands-on experience with a design system and began relying more on product metrics to support and validate design decisions.
